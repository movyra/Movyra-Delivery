# Getting Support for Movyra

Welcome to the Movyra support infrastructure. Whether you are an open-source contributor, a community developer, or an enterprise client utilizing Movyra by Bongo (an initiative of AnyAstro Techno Pvt Ltd), we provide comprehensive channels to assist you.

## Community Support Channels
For general architectural questions, implementation advice, integration help, or sharing custom Movyra builds, please utilize our community-driven platforms:

* **GitHub Discussions:** The primary venue for Q&A, architectural debates, feature requests, and showcasing integrations.
* **Discord Server:** Join the `AnyAstro Developers` Discord server for real-time chat, debugging assistance, and networking with other contributors.
* **Stack Overflow:** Ask specific technical questions using the tags `#movyra` and `#bongo-os` to reach the broader developer community.

## Filing Bug Reports
If you have identified a reproducible bug in the Movyra codebase, please follow these steps to ensure a swift resolution:
1.  Search the existing GitHub Issues tracker to confirm the bug has not already been reported or resolved.
2.  If the issue is novel, open a new ticket using the `ISSUE_TEMPLATE_BUG.md` template provided in the repository.
3.  You must provide minimal, reproducible code, exact UI steps, browser context, and relevant console logs to trigger the failure. Incomplete reports may be closed automatically.

## Enterprise Support Tier
Clients utilizing Movyra Enterprise Edition are entitled to dedicated, priority support in accordance with their executed Service Level Agreement (SLA).

* **Dedicated Enterprise Portal:** Authenticate via your AnyAstro Enterprise Portal to submit high-priority technical tickets and feature requests directly to our engineering team.
* **Direct Email Support:** Contact your dedicated account manager or email `enterprise-support@anyastro.com`.
* **SEV-1 Emergency Hotline:** For critical production outages (e.g., total dispatch routing failure, widespread authentication outages), please immediately call the 24/7 emergency hotline provided in your enterprise onboarding documentation.

## Documentation
Prior to opening any support ticket, please consult the official documentation. Complex configuration, environment variable setup, and deployment topologies are extensively documented in `DEVELOPMENT.md` and `DEPLOYMENT.md`.
